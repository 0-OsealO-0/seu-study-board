## 核心词汇

### 1. acclaim /əˈkleɪm/ n.

praise and approval for sb/sth, especially an artistic achievement （尤其对艺术成就的）称誉，高度评价
- She has won acclaim for her commitment to democracy.
  - 她为民主做出的贡献为她赢得了赞誉。
- The scientist earned acclaim for his remarkable achievement in the field.
  - 科学家以其在研究领域的突出成就而赢得了高度评价。

### 2. aftermath /ˈæf.tɚ.mæθ/ n.

the situation that exists as a result of an important (and usually unpleasant) event, especially a war, an accident, etc. 后果，余波
- We all worked together in the aftermath of the earthquake.
  - 我们在震后废墟中一起并肩作战。
- For a county still dealing with the aftermath of decades of corruption, we can do better.
  - 作为一个仍在致力于解决几十年的腐败隐患的国家来说，我们理应做的更好。

### 3. allocation /æl.əˈkeɪ.ʃən/ n.

the act of giving sth to sb for a particular purpose 划;拨;分配
- Higher budgetary allocations for education mean more teachers can be hired.
  - 高涨的教育经费拨款说明学校计划招聘更多教师。
- A huge amount of economic activity might be lost to inefficient allocation.
  - 大量的经济活动有可能因为低效率的财政分配而丧失效果。

### 4. combat /kɑːm.bæt/ v. / n.

a fight, especially during a war斗争，战争
- The film explores the combat between good and evil.
  - 这部电影针对正邪两方的斗争展开探索。
- I have to combat this constant desire to eat chocolate.
  - 我必须要抵抗住常常想吃巧克力的冲动。

### 5. coordination /koʊˌɔːr.dənˈeɪ.ʃən/ n.

the act of making parts of sth, groups of people, etc. work together in an efficient and organized way 协作；协调；配合
- There's absolutely no coordination between the different groups - nobody knows what anyone else is doing.
  - 不同的群体之间完全没有配合—每个人都不知道别人在做什么。
- We need better coordination between state and local authorities.
  - 我们需要来自国家和本地政府之间更好的协调配合。

### 6. derail /diːˈreɪl/ v.

to leave the track; to make a train do this （使）脱轨，出轨
- Investors are very concerned that a new crisis could derail the economic recovery.
  - 投资商十分担忧新一轮的危机会使得经济复苏难以为继。
- Companies should help women whose careers have often been derailed by maternity leave.
  - 对于因休产假而事业受到影响的女员工，公司有义务对其伸出援手。

### 7. destabilize /diːˈsteɪ.bəl.aɪz/ v.

to make a system, country, government, etc. become less firmly established or successful使（制度、国家、政府等）动摇；使不安定；使不稳定
- The bordering countries are being destabilized by the conflict.
  - 周边国家因战争冲突而受到干扰。
- Further increases in imports could destabilize the economy.
  - 进口产品的持续增长可能会扰乱经济。

### 8. disintegration /dɪs.ɪn.t̬əˈɡreɪ.ʃən/ n.

the process of losing cohesion or strength分裂解体
- She survived the disintegration of her marriage and the challenge of raising five children.
  - 她从离婚的创伤中恢复了过来，并且养育了五个孩子。
- Europeans who believe that disintegration is a good idea should consult the history of the 1920s and 1930s.
  - 那些支持分裂的欧洲人应该参考20和30年代的历史。

### 9. estimation n.

a judgement or opinion about the value or quality of sb/sth 判断；评价；看法
- His skills and abilities make him, in my estimation, an ideal candidate for the board of governors.
  - 在我看来，他的技能和能力使他成为理事会的理想人选。
- He sank in my estimation when I saw how he treated his wife.
  - 当我看到他如何对待他的妻子时，他在我心目中的形象一落千丈。

常用搭配：in somebody’s estimation；go up/come down in somebody’s estimation (=be respected or admired more or less by someone)

### 10. exceed v.

to be greater than a particular number or amount 超过（数量）
- The success of our campaign has exceeded our wildest expectations.
  - 我们发起的运动获得了意想不到的成功。
- She was found guilty on three charges of exceeding the speed limit.
  - 她被指控3次超速驾驶，并被判有罪。

### 11. expound v.

to explain sth by talking about it in detail 详解；详述；阐述
- He's always expounding on what's wrong with the world.
  - 他总在痛陈这个世界的弊病。
- She uses her newspaper column to expound her views on environmental issues.
  - 她利用主笔的报纸专栏阐述自己对环境问题的观点。

### 12. integration n.

the act or process of combining two or more things so that they work together (= of integrating them) 结合；整合；一体化
- His music is an integration of tradition and new technology.
  - 他的音乐结合了传统和新技术。
- To promote integration, several schools' catchment areas were merged.
  - 为了促进融合，合并了几所学校的学区。

常用搭配：integration of；integration into；racial integration

### 13. livelihood n.

a means of earning money in order to live 赚钱謀生的手段；生计
- Many ship workers could lose their livelihoods because of falling orders for new ships.
  - 由于新船订单减少，许多船厂工人可能要丢掉饭碗。
- Fishing is the main source of livelihood for many people in the area.
  - 捕鱼是该地区许多人的主要生计来源。

### 14. materialize v.

to take place or start to exist as expected or planned 实现；发生；成为现实
- Her hopes of becoming a painter never materialized.
  - 她当画家的愿望一直未实现。
- Suddenly a lorry appeared in front of her - it seemed to materialize out of nowhere.
  - 一辆卡车突然出现在她面前——好似是从地下冒出来的。

### 15. pledge v.

to formally promise to give or do sth 保证给予（或做）；正式承诺；n. a serious or formal promise, especially one to give money or to be a friend, or something that you give as a sign that you will keep a promise誓言；诺言；保证
- Herrera pledged that his company would give aid to schools.
  - 埃雷拉承诺他的公司将为学校提供援助。
- We are asking people to pledge their support for our campaign.
  - 我们正请求人们承诺支持我们的运动。
- I give you this ring as a pledge of my everlasting love for you.
  - 我把这枚戒指送给你作为我永远爱你的信物。
- All the candidates have given/made pledges not to raise taxes if they are elected.
  - 所有的候选人都保证当选后不增税。

常用搭配：pledge something to something/somebody；pledge to do something；pledge that；pledge to do something；make/take/give a pledge；keep/fulfil/honour a pledge

### 16. reaffirm v.

to state sth again in order to emphasize that it is still true 重申；再次确定
- The government yesterday reaffirmed its commitment to the current peace process.
  - 政府昨天重申了对目前和平进程的承诺。
- The government has reaffirmed that education is a top priority.
  - 政府重申教育是头等大事。

### 17. renew /rɪˈnjuː/ v.

to begin sth again after a pause or an interruption 使恢复，更新
- We have to renew our efforts to attract young players.
  - 我们只好重新开始努力吸引年轻队员。
- The annual dinner is a chance to renew acquaintance with old friends.
  - 一年一度的聚餐会是与老朋友叙旧的好机会。

### 18. solidify /səˈlɪdɪfaɪ/ v.

to become or to make sth become more definite and less likely to change （使）变得坚定，变得稳固，巩固
- Vague objections to the system solidified into firm opposition.
  - 先前对这一制度不大明朗的反对演变成了坚决的抵制。
- They solidified their position as Britain's top band.
  - 他们巩固了自己作为英国顶尖乐队的地位。

### 19. stabilize /ˈsteɪbəlaɪz/ v.

to become or to make sth become firm, steady and unlikely to change; to make sth stable （使）稳定，稳固
- The patient's condition stabilized.
  - 患者的病情稳定下来。
- Doctors stabilized the patient's condition.
  - 大夫们使患者的病情稳定下来。

### 20. substantial /səbˈstænʃ(ə)l/ a.

large in amount, value or importance 大量的；价值巨大的；重大的
- Substantial numbers of people support the reforms.
  - 相当多的人支持这些改革措施。
- Your claim ought to succeed, in which case the damages will be substantial.
  - 你的索赔应当能成功，假如这样的话，损害赔偿金将会相当可观。

### 21. violation /ˌvaɪəˈleɪʃ(ə)n/ n.

the action of violating something 违反，违背；侵权
- To deprive the boy of his education is a violation of state law.
  - 剥夺这个男孩的受教育权是一种违反州法律的行为。
- The judge called the decision "a flagrant violation of international law."
  - 法官称这个决定是“对国际法的公然违反”。

### 22. abide by /əˈbaɪd baɪ/

to accept and act according to a law, an agreement, etc. 遵守，遵照（法规、约定等）
- You'll have to abide by the rules of the club.
  - 你必须遵守俱乐部的规定。
- We are not free agents; we abide by the decisions of our president.
  - 我们不是自由不需负责任的人；我们遵守总统的决定。

### 23. hinge on /hɪndʒ ɒn/

to depend on sth completely 有赖于；取决于
- The plan hinges on a deal being struck with a new company.
  - 该计划完全取决于正在和一家新公司谈的一笔交易。
- His success hinges on how well he does at the interview.
  - 他能否成功要看他在面试中的表现。

### 24. in conformity with /ɪn kənˈfɔːməti wɪð/

following the rules of sth; conforming to sth 遵循（规则）；与…相符合（或一致）
- These regulations are in conformity with European law.
  - 这些规章符合欧洲法律。
- The prime minister is, in conformity with their constitution, chosen by the president.
  - 首相是遵照宪法由总统选定的。

### 25. scale up /skeɪl ʌp/

to increase the size or number of sth 增大，扩大（规模或数量）
- All in all, it's a great business that should be easy to scale up.
  - 总之，这是一项很好的业务，应该很容易扩大规模。
- Agricultural commodity producers should be supported to diversify and scale up their production.
  - 我们应该支持农产品生产主体的多元化规模化发展。

## 复习方式

1. 先按英文回忆中文含义，再反向检查中译英。
2. 重点记录老师已布置练习中的错题，不重复生成额外练习题。
3. 结合课文语境复习一词多义、固定搭配和短语中的介词。
