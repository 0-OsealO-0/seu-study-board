## 三、课堂核心词（13）

### 1. commendation /ˌkɒmenˈdeɪʃn/ n.

an award or official statement giving public praise for sb / sth（奖品；奖励；表扬）

- She received a commendation for her outstanding work.
  她因工作出色而受到嘉奖。
- The soldier was awarded a commendation for bravery.
  这位士兵因英勇表现被授予嘉奖。

### 2. proceed /prəˈsiːd/ v.

to continue doing something or move forward（注意与 precede “在……之前发生”区分）（继续做（或从事、进行））

- Please proceed to the next checkpoint.
  请继续前往下一个检查点。
- The meeting will proceed as scheduled.
  会议将按计划进行。

### 3. articulate /ɑːˈtɪkjuleɪt/ v.

to express ideas clearly in speech or writing（明确表达；清楚说明）

- He struggled to articulate his thoughts during the debate.
  他在辩论中难以清晰表达观点。
- A good teacher can articulate complex concepts simply.
  优秀的教师能将复杂概念讲解得通俗易懂。

### 4. attendance /əˈtendəns/ n.

the act of being present at an event（出席；参加；上学；到场）

- Student attendance at lectures is mandatory.
  学生必须出席讲座。
- The concert had record attendance this year.
  今年音乐会出席人数创下纪录。

### 5. dedication /ˌdedɪˈkeɪʃn/ n.

the hard work and effort that sb puts into an activity or purpose because they think it is important（献身；奉献）

- Her dedication to the project ensured its success.
  她对项目的奉献确保了其成功。
- The team’s dedication impressed the coach.
  球队的专注态度令教练印象深刻。

### 6. devastating /ˈdevəsteɪtɪŋ/ adj.

extremely shocking to a person; causing severe harm or destruction（令人震惊的；骇人的；毁灭性的）

- The hurricane had a devastating impact on the coastal town.
  飓风对沿海小镇造成了毁灭性影响。
- His criticism was both accurate and devastating.
  他的批评既一针见血又令人难堪。

### 7. expenditure /ɪkˈspendɪtʃə/ n.

the act of spending or using money; an amount of money spent（花费；消费；费用；开支）

- Government expenditure on healthcare has increased.
  政府在医疗保健上的支出增加了。
- We need to reduce unnecessary expenditures.
  我们需要减少不必要的开支。

### 8. legitimacy /lɪˈdʒɪtɪməsi/ n.

conformity to the law or to rules; the quality of being acceptable（合法性；合理性）

- The election’s legitimacy was questioned by international observers.
  国际观察员质疑选举的合法性。
- Protesters challenged the legitimacy of the new law.
  抗议者质疑新法的正当性。

### 9. lobby /ˈlɒbi/ v.

to try to persuade a politician or the government to support or oppose a change in the law（游说（从政者或政府））

- Environmental groups lobbied for stricter pollution controls.
  环保组织游说推行更严格的污染管控。
- The industry lobbied against the proposed tax increase.
  该行业游说反对拟议的增税。

### 10. mobilize /ˈməʊbəlaɪz/ v.

to organize resources or people for action（组织；鼓动；动员）

- The government mobilized troops to respond to the crisis.
  政府动员军队应对危机。
- Volunteers mobilized quickly to help flood victims.
  志愿者迅速动员起来帮助洪灾受害者。

### 11. optimal /ˈɒptɪməl/ adj.

best or most favorable / most effective（最佳的；最适的）

- The optimal temperature for plant growth is 25°C.
  植物生长的最佳温度是 25 摄氏度。
- We aim to achieve optimal efficiency in production.
  我们的目标是实现生产效益最大化。

### 12. perception /pəˈsepʃn/ n.

an idea, a belief or an image you have as a result of how you see or understand sth（看法；见解；感知）

- Public perception of the policy has shifted recently.
  公众对这项政策的看法最近有所转变。
- Cultural differences influence our perception of time.
  文化差异影响我们对时间的感知。

### 13. sustain /səˈsteɪn/ v.

to make sth continue for some time without becoming less; to maintain or support（使保持；使稳定持续；维持）

- The economy must sustain steady growth.
  经济必须保持稳定增长。
- His argument was sustained by solid evidence.
  他的论点有确凿证据支撑。

## 七、语言点（9）

### LP1 现在分词短语作伴随状语（demonstrating …）

> Organizations and networks of young people have emerged at the local, national, regional and global levels, demonstrating their capacity for advocacy, communication and negotiation, and their commitment to challenging injustice.（Lines 4–6, Para. 1）

- demonstrating … 是现在分词短语作伴随状语，逻辑主语是句子的主语 organizations and networks of young people；分词短语说明主句动作“出现”所带来的表现。
- 非谓语动词（non-finite verb）没有语气、时态、数和人称的变化，不能单独作谓语；本句真正的谓语是 have emerged。
- 可改写为定语从句：… which demonstrate their capacity for advocacy, communication and negotiation, and their commitment to challenging injustice。
- 写作可套用：X have emerged at …, demonstrating their capacity for … and their commitment to …。
- *They are renowned for their advocacy of family values.* 他们以提倡家庭价值观而闻名。
- *A career as an actor requires one hundred percent commitment.* 干演员这一行需要百分之百的投入。

### LP2 yet 转折 + 六个 wh- 宾语从句并列

> Most Governments are concerned about improving young people’s educational experience, yet very few take any measures to find out from students themselves which teaching methods work, whether the curriculum is relevant, what factors contribute to school dropout rates and truancy, how to improve attendance rates, what is needed to promote better inclusion of girls, or how to enhance good behavior and promote effective discipline.（Lines 2–9, Para. 3）

- yet 在此作连词，表示“然而、可是”，常与否定意味的后半句连用，相当于 nevertheless；它让“政府很关心”与“几乎不采取行动”形成反差与讽刺。
- find out 后接六个并列的宾语从句：which teaching methods work / whether the curriculum is relevant / what factors contribute to … / how to improve … / what is needed … / how to enhance …，长短交错、节奏由短到长。
- curriculum n. the subjects included in a course of study（学校等的）全部课程；复数 curricula / curriculums。
- contribute to：to be one of the causes of sth 是……的原因之一；注意 to 是介词，后接名词或动名词。
- inclusion n. the act of making a person or thing part of a group 包括；反义 exclusion。
- enhance v. to increase or further improve the good quality, value or status of sb / sth 提高；增强，比 improve 更正式。
- truancy n. the practice of staying away from school without permission 旷课；逃学（形容词 truant）。
- *Teachers incorporated business skills into the regular school curriculum.* 教师们将商业技能纳入学校常规课程。
- *The report says design faults in both the vessels contributed to the tragedy.* 报道称两艘船在设计上的缺陷是造成悲剧的原因。
- *This is an opportunity to enhance the reputation of the company.* 这是提高公司声誉的机会。

### LP3 that 宾语从句 + in which 定语从句（= where）

> Evidence shows that schools in which democratic environments are introduced are likely to have a more harmonious atmosphere, better staff-student relationships and a more effective learning environment.（Lines 11–13, Para. 3）

- Evidence shows that … 中 that 引导宾语从句（名词性从句），作 shows 的宾语。
- schools in which democratic environments are introduced 是定语从句修饰 schools；关系副词 in which 相当于 where，schools 是先行词。
- 主句是 schools are likely to have A, B and C 三个并列宾语，比较级 more harmonious / better / more effective 层层递进。
- democratic adj. based on the idea that everyone should have equal rights and should be involved in making important decisions 民主精神的。
- harmonious adj. friendly, peaceful and without any disagreement 和谐的（名词 harmony）。
- *Education is the basis of a democratic society.* 教育是民主社会的基础。
- *Their harmonious relationship resulted in part from their similar goals.* 他们融洽的关系部分来源于他们相似的目标。

### LP4 as 时间状语从句 + divergent / diverse / mobility

> As youth lifestyles become more divergent and the rate of change increases, and as populations grow more diverse through immigration and mobility, it becomes ever more difficult for those in authority to adequately understand youth.（Lines 1–3, Para. 4）

- 两个 as 引导并列的时间状语从句，表示“随着……”；主句用 it becomes … for sb to do sth 形式主语结构。
- divergent 与 diverse 近义但不同：divergent 强调“正在分岔、方向不同”，差异会随时间扩大；diverse 强调“种类繁多、彼此不同”，差异一眼可见。
- mobility n. the ability to move easily from one place, social class, or job to another（住处、社会阶层、职业方面的）流动能力。
- those in authority 当权者；adequately adv. 充分地——两个副词性表达让批评语气克制而有分量。
- *Two people who have divergent views on this question are George Watt and Bob Marr.* 在这个问题上意见相左的两个人是乔治·瓦特和鲍勃·马尔。
- *Recent years have witnessed a growing social mobility.* 近年来人们的社会流动性越来越大。

### LP5 强调句 It is … that … + take sth seriously

> It is by questioning, expressing their views and having their opinions taken seriously that young people develop skills, build competencies, acquire confidence and form aspirations.（Lines 2–5, Para. 5）

- It is … that … 是强调句型，被强调的部分是方式状语 by questioning, expressing their views and having their opinions taken seriously；去掉 It is 与 that 后句子仍完整。
- having their opinions taken seriously 是 have sth done 结构（让别人认真对待自己的意见），taken seriously 是 take sb / sth seriously 的被动形式。
- develop skills, build competencies, acquire confidence and form aspirations 四个动宾搭配层层递进，是写作的好模板。
- competence n. the ability to do sth well 能力，胜任（~ in sth / ~ in doing sth）。
- aspiration n. a strong desire to have or do sth 渴望；抱负；志向（常与 for 或 to do 连用）。
- *The players displayed varying degrees of competence.* 运动员们显示了不同程度的能力。
- *I didn’t realize you had political aspirations.* 我没有意识到你有政治上的抱负。

### LP6 There is … demonstrating that … + afford

> There is a considerable body of evidence demonstrating that young people who are afforded opportunities for meaningful participation within their families and communities are more likely to achieve healthy development.（Lines 8–11, Para. 5）

- There is a considerable body of evidence demonstrating that … 是“存在句 + 现在分词后置定语 + that 同位语从句”的复合结构；a body of 表示“大量的（证据、知识等）”。
- who are afforded opportunities for meaningful participation 是定语从句修饰 young people，afford 在此意为“提供、给予”。
- afford v. to provide sb with sth 提供；给予（afford sb sth）；另一常用义“负担得起”。
- be more likely to do sth 更有可能做某事，学术写作中表达相关性的稳妥说法。
- *The programme affords young people the chance to gain work experience.* 这项计划给年轻人提供了获得工作经验的机会。
- *There is a growing body of evidence linking diet to health.* 有越来越多的证据表明饮食与健康有关。

### LP7 过去分词短语作后置定语（committed to …）

> A young person committed to principles of human rights and a belief in the effectiveness of peer counselling founded the Philadelphia Student Union to create opportunities for young people to make a difference.（Lines 5–8, Para. 6）

- committed to principles of human rights and a belief in the effectiveness of peer counselling 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰主语 a young person；主句谓语是 founded。
- committed adj. willing to work hard and give your time and energy to sth; believing strongly in sth 尽心尽力的；坚信的；坚定的。
- commit oneself to sth 使自己致力于／投身于；be committed to 致力于（to 为介词）。
- peer counselling 朋辈心理咨询；朋辈辅导。make a difference 有影响；起作用。
- Philadelphia Student Union（PSU）是费城以学生为中心的组织，旨在为学生争取高质量教育。
- *They are committed socialists.* 他们是坚定的社会主义者。
- *I would advise people to think carefully about committing themselves to working Sundays.* 我会建议人们认真考虑答应周日工作。

### LP8 Whether … or … 让步状语 + impose / entitle

> Whether it is an issue relating to rules imposed at school, legislation on the minimum age for full-time work, representation of young people in the media, or priorities in public expenditure, youth are entitled to articulate their concerns, participate in the development of policy and have their opinions given serious consideration.（Lines 2–6, Para. 7）

- Whether it is A, B, C, or D 是让步状语从句，意为“无论是……还是……”；主句在后，四个并列项覆盖学校、立法、媒体与财政四个领域。
- relating to … 现在分词短语作后置定语；rules imposed at school 中 imposed 是过去分词作后置定语。
- impose v. (usually with on) to use your authority to force people to accept sth 强制实行；impose sth on sb。
- entitle v. (often passive) entitle sb to sth 使享有权利；使符合资格；be entitled to do sth 有权做某事。
- have their opinions given serious consideration 是 have sth done 结构，与 LP5 的 taken seriously 同类。
- *Fines are imposed on retailers who sell tobacco to minors.* 向未成年人销售烟草制品的零售商要被强制罚款。
- *Everyone’s entitled to their own opinion.* 人人都有权发表自己的意见。

### LP9 Only then + 部分倒装

> Only then will the traditional approaches toward youth begin to evolve and the oft-stated commitment to their participation begin to have meaning.（Lines 8–10, Para. 9）

- only + 状语（only then / only by doing … / only when …）置于句首时，主句用部分倒装：把助动词或情态动词提到主语前。本句将 will 提到 traditional approaches 之前。
- 两个并列主语 the traditional approaches toward youth 与 the oft-stated commitment to their participation 共用同一个谓语 will begin。
- approach n. (usually with to) a way of dealing with sb / sth（待人接物或思考问题的）方式，方法，态度。
- oft-stated adj. frequently stated 经常说的（oft 为古体／书面副词，构成复合形容词）。
- *The school has decided to adopt a different approach to discipline.* 学校决定采取另外一种方式解决纪律问题。
- *His oft-stated aim is to cut taxes.* 他经常说的目标是减税。

## 八、课文参考译文（9 段）

### Para. 1

年轻人参与决策

目前，世界上每个地区都有年轻人参与决策的倡议、项目和计划。许多年轻人开始塑造他们周围的世界，以自己独特的视角影响政治家、决策者、专业人士和媒体。年轻人的组织和网络出现在地方、国家、区域和全球范围，展示了他们倡导、沟通和谈判的能力，以及挑战不公正现象的决心。

### Para. 2

年轻人有大量独特的体验，他们的观点和看法都源自这种体验。他们是拥有技能和能力的社会行动者，对自己的问题可以提出建设性的解决方案。然而，有时，人们未能或拒绝承认年轻人在计划、政策、决策制定等方面所做贡献的合理性／合法性。

### Para. 3

有一个跟教育相关的政策采用和实施脱节的例子。大多数政府都对改善年轻人的教育体验感到关切，但几乎没有政府会采取措施从学生身上发现哪种教学方法会奏效，课程设置是否和实际需求相关，哪些因素会影响学校的辍学率和逃学人数，如何提高出勤率，需要做些什么来吸纳更多的女孩上学，或者怎么使学生表现得更好，以及如何加强有效的纪律管理。

儿童权利委员会一贯要求各国政府解释在本国的学校体制中，学生的参与是如何实现的，但这方面的进展极为缓慢。有证据表明，引入民主氛围的学校可能有更和谐的氛围、更好的师生关系和更有效的学习环境。如果要降低世界上多国出现的惊人辍学率，教育管理者和政策制定者需要向儿童和年轻人学习，知道学习机构怎样才能成为他们想要的地方。要让他们对学校有主人翁意识和责任感，就得给他们机会，让他们参与到日常可能影响到他们的决定、政策、组织等相关事务中去。

### Para. 4

随着年轻人的生活方式越来越多样化，变化的速度越来越快，以及移民和流动性带来的人口多样化，权威人士更难充分了解年轻人。对于发展有效的公共政策和获取积极的成果而言，征求年轻人的看法，利用他们的知识，吸收他们的想法是有必要的。

### Para. 5

参与可以提升年轻人的幸福，促进他们的发展。年轻人通过提出问题、表达看法、让他人严肃对待其意见来培养技能、锻造能力、获取自信、立下壮志。这是一个良性循环。一个年轻人有更多机会参与有意义的事务，他／她就会变得更有经验，能力也更强。这使他／她的参与更加有效，进而促进发展。很多证据表明，在家庭和社区里有机会参与有意义的事务的年轻人更有可能获得健康的发展。从广义上讲，参与也是实现发展的一种手段。阿马蒂亚·森认为，发展是“扩大人们享有的真正自由的过程”。严格来讲，发展包括实现健康、教育和经济增长领域的特定目标，但最终目的是让人们能够选择并过上他们想要的生活。因此，一个发达的社区是一个允许其所有成员参与的社区。

### Para. 6

费城公立学校采取的行动为良性循环效应提供了证明。该地区的学校曾经具有贫困、物质匮乏、充斥不满情绪、高辍学率和种族关系紧张等特点。一位致力于人权原则并相信朋辈辅导有效性的年轻人成立了费城学生会，为年轻人创造改变现状的机会。学生会的具体目标包括让学生参与决策、结束虐待、促进多元文化教育以及开发互动性强且引人入胜的课程。五年后，学生会已动员了 500 多名成员且取得了诸多成就，其中有为建立一个新的学校资助系统进行游说，并成功将一项削减经费的提议转化为向学区拨款 1 500 万美元；它通过说服教师为重新设计的专业发展进行游说，得到了改善学校教学的承诺。

它还成功地敦促学校进行建筑检查并进行必要的维修。最重要的是，它让成年人意识到，当给予学生一定的信任让他们来帮忙改善自己的生活环境时，年轻人可以学习得更好。

### Para. 7

参与是一项基本人权。包括年轻人在内的所有人都有权对直接影响他们生活的决策发表意见。无论是涉及学校规定、全职工作最低年龄立法、年轻人在媒体中的代表性，还是公共支出优先事项的问题，年轻人都有权表达自己的关切，参与政策制定，使其意见受到认真考虑。

参与是年轻人为自己争取权利和改变处境的一种手段。自 1985 年“国际青年年”以来，联合国大会将年轻人的参与定义为四个组成部分：经济参与（与工作和发展有关）、政治参与（与决策过程有关）、社会参与（与社区参与有关）和文化参与（与艺术、文化价值观和表达有关）。《儿童权利公约》重申了这四个要素，它们是创造尊重儿童与青年的文化的核心。

### Para. 8

应当积极努力，听取年轻人的意见，使他们投身于加强参与式民主的进程中。年轻人的参与可以带来更好的决策和结果，促进他们的福祉和发展，加强他们对人权的承诺和理解，并为他们提供一种保护，也可以让他们参与到决策制定的全过程。

### Para. 9

年轻人的参与是确保年轻人最佳发展的重要战略，也是实现社会更广泛发展目标的重要战略。迄今为止，在促进参与方面所取得的进展应该得到保持和加强。年轻人的参与必须成为地方、国家和国际青年政策的一个组成部分，并为影响儿童和青年日常生活的决策和行动提供框架。

只有这样，对待年轻人的传统方式才会开始演变，人们经常说的令年轻人参与的承诺才会变得有意义。这种做法必须提倡对年轻人的尊重，将他们视为社会的行动者、自己生活的代理人以及自己社会的公民。

## 九、Text Comprehension 答案

### Para. 1

**1. On what aspects are young people exerting their influence?（提示词：独特的视角／政治家／决策者／专业人士／媒体）**

答：They are shaping the world around them and influencing **politicians, decision-makers, professionals and the media** with **a unique perspective**. （原句：Many young people are already shaping the world around them, influencing politicians, decision-makers, professionals and the media with a unique perspective.）

**2. What do young people in organizations or networks at different levels showcase?（提示词：拥护、沟通和协商／挑战不公正现象）**

答：They showcase their **capacity for advocacy, communication and negotiation**, and their **commitment to challenging injustice**. （原句：… demonstrating their capacity for advocacy, communication and negotiation, and their commitment to challenging injustice.）

### Para. 2

**1. What’s the possible obstacle to young people’s participation in solving their own problems?（提示词：大量的／源自／建设性的解决方案）**

答：Young people hold **a body of** unique experience, from which their views and perceptions **derive**, and they can propose **constructive resolutions**; yet sometimes people **fail or refuse to recognize the legitimacy of their contribution** to plans, policies and decision-making. That refusal is the obstacle. （原句：They are social actors with skills and competencies, offering constructive resolutions to their own problems. Yet, at times, people fail or refuse to recognize the legitimacy of the contribution that young people make to plans, policies and decision-making.）

### Para. 3

**1. What are the problems that most governments fail to take measures to make clear?**

答：Which **teaching methods work**, whether the **curriculum is relevant**, what factors contribute to **school dropout rates and truancy**, how to **improve attendance rates**, what is needed to promote **better inclusion of girls**, and how to **enhance good behavior and promote effective discipline**.

**2. How are schools featuring democratic environments?**

答：They are likely to have **a more harmonious atmosphere**, **better staff-student relationships** and **a more effective learning environment**.

**3. If we want to reduce student dropout rates, schools should be made a place where ____________ and which they ____________ and ____________. How can we achieve this?**

答：… a place where **children and young people want to be**, and which they **have a sense of ownership in** and **responsibility for**. To achieve this, they must be **given opportunities to participate in the decisions, policies and organizations that affect them on a daily basis**. （原句：… how learning institutions can become the places children and young people want them to be. To give them a sense of ownership in and responsibility for school, they must be given opportunities to participate in the decisions, policies, organizations and so on that affect them on a daily basis.）

### Para. 4

**1. What causes the understanding gap between the authorities and the youth?（提示词：人口流动／多样化／变化速度）**

答：Youth lifestyles become **more divergent**, the **rate of change increases**, and populations grow **more diverse through immigration and mobility**; it therefore becomes ever more difficult for **those in authority** to adequately understand youth.

**2. What can the authorities do to bridge the gap?（提示词：采纳（利用）某人的见解）**

答：They should **seek young people’s views**, **draw on their knowledge** and **incorporate their perceptions** into the development of effective public policy.

### Para. 5

**1. What’s the virtuous circle to enhance the youth’s development? A. More ____________ for meaningful participation B. More ____________ and ____________ C. More ____________ ____________**

答：A. More **opportunities** for meaningful participation → B. More **experience** and **competence** → C. More **effective participation**, which in turn promotes development（良性循环）.…… （原句：A young person who has more opportunities to participate in meaningful activities becomes more experienced and more competent. This makes his or her participation more effective and in turn promotes development.）

**2. What does comprehensive development include?**

答：It includes **the realization of particular goals in the areas of health, education and economic growth**, while the ultimate aim is to enable people **to choose and live the life they want**.

**3. What’s “the real freedom”?**

答：In Amartya Sen’s view, development is **the process of expanding the real freedoms that people enjoy**; therefore **a developed community is one that allows all its members to participate**.

### Para. 6

**1. What kind of a young person is the founder of the PSU?（提示词：致力于；坚信／朋辈辅导）**

答：A young person **committed to principles of human rights** and **a belief in the effectiveness of peer counselling**.

**2. What has the PSU achieved?（提示词：种族紧张／具体目标／游说／信任某人做某事）**

答：In the district once marked by poverty, deprivation, disaffection, high dropout rates and **racial tension**, the PSU set **specific objectives** and, five years later, had **mobilized more than 500 members**: it **lobbied for** a new school funding system and turned a proposed budget cut into **$15 million for the district**; it secured **a commitment to improve school teaching** by persuading teachers to lobby for redesigned professional development; it urged schools **to conduct building inspections and make necessary repairs**; and most importantly, it made adults realize that **young people can learn better when they are trusted** to help improve their own environment.

### Para. 7

**1. Youth have the right to express their concerns, participate in policy development and give serious consideration to the opinions. ( T / F )**

答：**F（False）**。原文是 youth are entitled to … **have their opinions given serious consideration**——年轻人有权让自己的意见**被**认真考虑，而不是由他们去考虑别人的意见。

**2. Comprehensive youth participation consists of ______, ______, ______ and ______ participation which can help create a culture of ______ for youth.**

答：**economic**, **political**, **social** and **cultural** participation（经济、政治、社会、文化参与），which can help create **a culture of respect** for children and youth.

### Para. 8

**1. What are the positive benefits of youth participation?（提示词：努力做某事／加强参与式民主）**

答：Participation leads to **better decisions and outcomes**, promotes **their well-being and development**, strengthens **their commitment to and understanding of human rights**, provides them **with a form of protection**, and lets them take part in **the whole process of decision-making**. Efforts must be made to **strengthen participatory democracy**.

### Para. 9

**1. What roles should youth play in today’s society?（提示词：必要的组成部分／时常提及的承诺／促进对……的尊重）**

答：Youth should be regarded as **social actors**, **agents of their own lives** and **citizens of their own societies**. Participation must become **an integral component** of local, national and international youth policy and **provide the framework** for decisions and actions affecting children and youth; only then will the **oft-stated commitment** to their participation begin to have meaning and **promote respect for** young people.
